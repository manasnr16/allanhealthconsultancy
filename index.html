About Allan Health Consultancy

Your route to treatment, one clear step at a time

Every step happens in a fixed order, so nothing is booked before it is medically sound.

Start with step one
  1. 1

    Intimation

    Tell us about the condition, share your reports and name the city you prefer, by phone, WhatsApp or the contact form.

  2. 2

    Free preliminary consultation

    We explain your options in Assamese, Bengali or English. Send your medical reports on WhatsApp for a free preliminary opinion.

  3. 3

    Second opinion

    Senior specialists review your case before you commit to surgery. If the treating doctor raises a concern, we arrange additional free consultations.

  4. 4

    Medical supervision sign-off

    Your case is reviewed by medical supervision so that every admission is medically appropriate and properly planned.

  5. 5Safeguard

    Hospital bed booking

    Only now do we confirm the bed. For every planned surgery, booking follows intimation, consultation or second opinion, and sign-off. Never before.

  6. 6

    Travel and stay

    Flights, accommodation, airport or station pickup and drop, local transport and an interpreter, all arranged before you leave home.

  7. 7

    Treatment and follow-up

    Your coordinator stays with you through treatment, then arranges follow-up once you are back home.
