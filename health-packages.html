Health check-up packages for patients travelling to Bangalore

A comprehensive check-up, a place to stay and a coordinator who handles the rest. Choose the package that fits your family.

What the check-up includes

Every package includes a Comprehensive Health Check-Up Program at a partner hospital.

  • Blood work
  • Cardiac screening
  • Diabetes panel
  • Physician consultation
  • Tests customised to age and risk factors. Ask our team.
  • A detailed report with doctor's recommendations, shared before you head home
