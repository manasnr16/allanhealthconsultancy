About Allan Health Consultancy: Your Trusted Bridge to World-Class Healthcare

For thousands of families across Assam, Meghalaya, Mizoram, West Bengal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and other SAARC nations, and for the Indian diaspora in the UK and USA, the biggest barrier to good treatment isn't the doctor. It's everything around the doctor.

Which hospital to trust, how to get an appointment quickly, where to stay, how to manage travel with a sick family member, and who to call when something goes wrong.

Allan Health Consultancy (operating as Allan Hospitality & Health Care Service) exists to remove that barrier. We are a Bangalore-based medical facilitation and patient-support company. We connect individuals and families from North East India, West Bengal, Bangladesh and all SAARC countries, as well as patients travelling from Sri Lanka, the UK and the USA, with leading multi-specialty hospitals in Bangalore, Chennai and Hyderabad. We stay with you at every step of that journey, not just the booking.

Our story

Allan Health Consultancy was founded out of a simple, personal observation: patients travelling long distances for specialised treatment were losing precious time and money not to their illness, but to logistics. Long waiting lists, unfamiliar cities, language barriers, and no one to guide them through unfamiliar hospital systems. We built a dedicated team and hospital network to solve exactly this problem, so patients can focus on getting better, not on navigating a new city.

What we do

We are not a hospital. We are your healthcare journey partner.

  • Priority, zero-waiting-time appointment bookings at partner hospitals
  • Expert second-opinion consultations before major treatment decisions
  • End-to-end coordination for planned surgeries: cardiac, orthopedic, oncology, IVF, gynecology and more
  • Free preliminary consultations to help you understand your options
  • Travel, accommodation, airport or station pickup and drop, and local transport
  • Interpreter and language support in Assamese and Bengali (Bangla)
  • Post-treatment follow-up coordination once you return home
  • Access to robotic-assisted surgery options across all major specialties, where medically appropriate
  • Support for underprivileged patients in accessing quality treatment through our assistance programs

Why choose us

  • Access to leading hospitals across Bangalore, Chennai and Hyderabad
  • Personalised care plans: every patient is assigned a dedicated coordinator
  • Zero-waiting-time appointments through our direct hospital relationships
  • Expert second opinions from senior specialists before you commit to surgery, with additional free consultations arranged whenever the treating doctor raises a specific concern
  • Complete travel and stay assistance, including flights, accommodation and local transport
  • Transparent, upfront cost estimates with no hidden charges
  • Regional language support so nothing is lost in translation, medically or otherwise
  • Hospital bed booking is confirmed only after intimation, consultation or second-opinion review, and medical supervision sign-off, so every admission is medically appropriate and properly planned
  • A dedicated assistance track for underprivileged patients, connecting them with hospital charitable schemes and subsidised care wherever available

Whether you are coming for a routine health check-up or a complex planned surgery, our promise is simple: you should never have to navigate this alone. From the first phone call to your safe journey home, Allan Health Consultancy is with you.
