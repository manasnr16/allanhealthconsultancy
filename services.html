Services that carry the whole journey

From the first question to the follow-up back home, every service is designed around one idea: you should never have to navigate this alone.

For all planned surgeries, hospital bed booking is confirmed only after intimation, consultation or second opinion, and medical supervision sign-off, never before.

Free consultation

Understand your options before you travel a single kilometre.

  • A free preliminary consultation with our team
  • Send your medical reports on WhatsApp and get a free preliminary opinion
  • Explained in Assamese, Bengali or English
  • A dedicated coordinator assigned from the first call

Book a free consultation

Second opinion consultation

Expert second opinions before major treatment decisions.

  • Review by senior specialists before you commit to surgery
  • Additional free consultations arranged whenever the treating doctor raises a specific concern
  • Clear, upfront cost estimates with no hidden charges
  • Bed booking only after medical supervision sign-off

Ask for a second opinion

Health check-up packages

Preventive check-ups with stay, transport and a coordinator included.

  • Comprehensive programs with blood work, cardiac screening, diabetes panel and physician consultation
  • Tests customised to age and risk factors
  • Bundled with accommodation and pickup as Gift of Health coupons
  • Detailed report and doctor's recommendations before you head home

Compare the packages

Travel, visa & accommodation assistance

Everything between your front door and the hospital door.

  • Flights and accommodation arranged for you and your attendant
  • Airport and railway station pickup and drop, plus local transport
  • Interpreter and language support in Assamese and Bengali (Bangla)
  • Guidance for international patients on medical-visa documentation
